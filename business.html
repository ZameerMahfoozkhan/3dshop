Engineering Materials Guide

We match the right polymer to your mechanical, thermal, and cosmetic requirements.

PLA+ Concept / Aesthetic

High dimensional accuracy, crisp sharp edges, zero warping. Ideal for visual prototypes, architectural models, and consumer goods.

• Heat Deflection: ~55°C • Tensile Strength: Medium • Finish: Crisp Matte or Gloss
PETG Functional / Tough

High impact resistance, chemical resilience, and weather durability. Excellent for mechanical snap-fits, brackets, and outdoor use.

• Heat Deflection: ~75°C • Tensile Strength: High • Finish: Semi-Gloss / Satin
ABS / ASA Thermal & UV

Automotive grade durability. Resists prolonged direct sunlight and temperatures up to 95°C. Machinable, sandable, and threadable.

• Heat Deflection: ~95°C • Tensile Strength: High • Finish: Industrial Matte
Carbon Composite High Rigidity

Carbon-fiber micro-strands embedded into PETG or Nylon. Minimal flex, high torsional stiffness, and a stealth textured finish.

• Heat Deflection: ~85°C • Rigidity: Ultra-High • Finish: Textured Stealth Grey
