Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At 3D Shop, your privacy and the confidentiality of your intellectual property, sketches, and proprietary CAD files are treated with the highest integrity.

1. Zero Server-Side File Storage

Our website is a static catalog and enquiry portal. We do not maintain an automated file upload server or database where your 3D models are stored in the cloud. All sketches, reference images, and 3D files (STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP) are transmitted directly between you and our studio via end-to-end encrypted WhatsApp chats or direct email.

2. Information We Receive

When you contact us or place an order via WhatsApp, we receive only the details you voluntarily provide:

  • Your name and contact phone number.
  • Delivery address and postal pincode for courier dispatch.
  • Project specifications, dimensions, and material choices.

3. Confidentiality of 3D Models & IP

Any custom model, engineering prototype, or intellectual property you share with 3D Shop remains strictly your property. We will never sell, license, publicly display, or share your proprietary design files without your prior written authorization.

4. Payment & Financial Security

3D Shop does not collect or store credit card numbers or banking passwords on this website. Payments are arranged manually via official UPI QR codes or direct bank transfers upon confirmation of quotation.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

For any privacy inquiries or to request deletion of your chat records, contact us at orders@3dshop.in or via our WhatsApp channel.
