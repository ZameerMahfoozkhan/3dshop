Technical Recommendations For Best Print Results

Wall Thickness

Ensure minimum wall thickness is at least 1.2mm (ideally 2.0mm+ for functional parts) to avoid fragile perimeters.

Hole Tolerances

For tight press-fit pins or M3/M4/M5 machine screws, allow 0.2mm to 0.3mm diametrical clearance in your CAD model.

Watertight Mesh

Ensure STL exports are manifold (no non-manifold edges, flipped normals, or internal floating islands).
